About

I am a research-driven computer scientist and Erasmus Mundus scholar pursuing a Master's in Applied Cybersecurity through the CyberMACS joint programme — Year 1 at Kadir Has University (Istanbul), Year 2 at SRH Berlin.

My work sits at the intersection of cybersecurity, machine learning, and systems engineering. I have published three peer-reviewed papers applying CNN, VGG16, and ensemble models to medical diagnostics — and built a production RAG-based chatbot using LangChain and LLaMA 3.1.

I hold a full CCNA certification, hands-on SOC/SIEM experience (TryHackMe SOC Level 1), and practical industry exposure from internships at SIBEREUM and BTCL. I approach every system with a secure-by-default mindset.

Targeting roles in SOC analysis, cybersecurity consulting, and IT security engineering — based in Berlin from October 2026.
